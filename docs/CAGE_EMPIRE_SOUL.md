# CAGE EMPIRE — Soul (Prime Directive)

> **Status:** Permanent. This is the project's north star. Every
> feature, every system, every UI decision must be evaluated against
> this document. If a feature does not strengthen one of the 5 pillars
> below, it is probably not worth building.
> **Source:** User-authored design philosophy, integrated 2026-07-21.

---

## The Core Fantasy

Every great management game has a fantasy.

- Football Manager: "I am a genius football manager."
- Civilization: "I am building a civilization through history."
- Crusader Kings: "I am creating a dynasty."
- OOTP: "I am building a baseball organization."

The Cage Empire fantasy is **not** "I run fight cards." That's admin work.

The fantasy is: **I am building an MMA empire and shaping the history
of the sport.**

Everything should reinforce that.

---

## The Real Dopamine Loop

Most coding AIs will build: scout → sign → book → get result → repeat.

That isn't enough. After 20 hours it becomes a spreadsheet simulator.

The real loop:

```
Discover → Invest → Develop → Promote → Watch Rise → Create Legacy → Shape History → Repeat
```

**The fighter is not the reward. The STORY is the reward.**

---

## What Players Actually Remember

Nobody remembers: `Striking = 87` five years later.

They remember: "That kid I found in Mexico. Nobody wanted him. He
became a champion. Retired undefeated. Now his son is fighting."

That's the memory. That's what we're building.

---

## The 5 Core Fantasies

### Fantasy 1 — Talent Hunter
**Player fantasy:** "I find greatness before anyone else."
**Systems:** scouting, hidden potential, uncertain reports, regional
networks, regen prospects.

### Fantasy 2 — Empire Builder
**Player fantasy:** "My promotion dominates the sport."
**Systems:** prestige, finances, market expansion, TV deals, champions.

### Fantasy 3 — Kingmaker
**Player fantasy:** "I create stars."
**Systems:** promotion, matchmaking, hype, rankings, media.

### Fantasy 4 — Historian
**Player fantasy:** "The world remembers what I built."
**Systems:** hall of fame, records, memories, historical comparisons,
legacy.

### Fantasy 5 — Puppet Master
**Player fantasy:** "The sport evolves because of my decisions."
**Systems:** rivalries, gym ecosystems, promotion ecosystems, career arcs.

---

## The Most Important KPI

**Do not measure success by: number of systems.**

**Measure success by: number of stories generated.**

Bad outcome: "New ranking algorithm added." Player doesn't care.

Good outcome: "36-year-old former champion returns after 4 years away.
Upsets undefeated prospect. Triggers a comeback storyline." Player
remembers forever.

---

## The Interpretation Layer's Real Purpose

Its purpose is not technical translation. Its purpose is:

**Translate simulation into emotion.**

Raw: `Age 37, Losses 4, Durability down 12%`
Meaning: `His best years may be behind him.`

That's what the player experiences.

---

## CAGE EMPIRE DESIGN LAW

> The player does not collect fighters.
> The player collects stories.
>
> Every major system must contribute to:
> 1. Discovery
> 2. Investment
> 3. Growth
> 4. Conflict
> 5. Legacy
>
> If a feature does not strengthen one of those five pillars,
> it is probably not worth building.

---

## Anticipation Is the Real Dopamine

Players should constantly have:
- The prospect I just signed.
- The champion nearing retirement.
- The rivalry exploding.
- The gym producing talent.
- The event next month.
- The young heavyweight everyone is talking about.

Something is always coming. Something is always developing. Something
is always unresolved.

That's what keeps people clicking Advance Day for another 500 hours.

Not the fight engine. Not the rankings. Not the finances. Those are
support systems.

The addiction comes from: **"I wonder what happens next."**

The Memory Engine, Career Arcs, Legacy System, and Interpretation
Layer are not just flavor — they are the machinery that creates
anticipation, attachment, and long-term stories.
